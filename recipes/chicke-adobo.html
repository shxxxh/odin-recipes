Chicken Adobo

Home

Description

Pork adobo is the unofficial national dish of the Philippines consisting of pork chunks slow-cooked in a rich, savory, and tangy reduction of soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves, and whole black peppercorns

Ingridients

  • Pork belly
  • garlic
  • Onion
  • Soy sauce
  • Salt
  • Dried bay leaves
  • vinegar

Instructions

  1. Combine the marinade: Mix 1/2 cup soy sauce, 1/2 cup cane or white vinegar, 1 whole head of smashed or minced garlic, 1 tsp whole black peppercorns, and 2–3 bay leaves in a bowl.
  2. Marinate the meat: Submerge 1kg of chicken thighs or pieces in the mixture and let it sit for at least 30 minutes (or overnight in the fridge for deeper flavor).
  3. Brown the chicken: Heat 1 tbsp of cooking oil in a pan, then sear the chicken pieces skin-side down until golden brown on both sides.
  4. Pour in the marinade: Add the leftover marinade, 1 cup of water, and 1 tbsp of brown sugar (optional) into the pan with the chicken.
  5. Simmer without stirring: Bring the liquid to a full boil, and do not stir for the first 5 minutes to let the raw vinegar cook off.
  6. Cook until tender: Cover the pan partially, lower the heat to a simmer, and cook for 20 to 30 minutes until the chicken is tender.
  7. Reduce and serve: Remove the lid and simmer on higher heat for 3 to 5 minutes until the sauce thickens into a rich glaze, then serve hot over steamed rice.
