Description

Pancit is a classic Filipino umbrella term for a wide variety of stir-fried or broth-based noodle dishes.a popular Filipino stir-fried flour-stick noodle dish inspired by Chinese chow mein and lo mein.

Ingridients

  • Noodles:
  • garlic
  • Onion
  • Soy sauce
  • Salt
  • Cabbage
  • Carrots

Instructions

  1. Sauté the aromatics and pepper: Heat cooking oil in a large wok or pot over medium heat, then sauté the minced garlic, sliced onions, and a dash of ground black pepper until the onions soften.
  2. Brown the proteins: Add your sliced pork, chicken breast, or shrimp into the pan and stir-fry for 3 to 4 minutes until the meat is lightly browned and cooked through.
  3. Toss in the vegetables: Add chopped cabbage, julienned carrots, and snap peas (or green beans) to the wok, then stir-fry them for about 3 to 4 minutes until crisp-tender. (Optional: You can remove the cooked meat and vegetables onto a side plate at this point so they don't overcook).
  4. Season with sauces: Pour in the soy sauce and oyster sauce, stirring well to coat the meat and vegetables.
  5. Pour the broth and boil: Pour in the chicken broth (or water with a bouillon cube) and bring the liquid to a rolling boil.
  6. Cook the noodles: Drop in the Pancit Canton flour stick noodles, cover or gently fold and toss them continuously until they absorb the broth and turn soft and tender (about 2 to 3 minutes).Cook the noodles: Drop in the Pancit Canton flour stick noodles, cover or gently fold and toss them continuously until they absorb the broth and turn soft and tender (about 2 to 3 minutes).
  7. Return your cooked meat and vegetables back into the pan (if you set them aside), toss everything together for a final minute, and serve hot with fresh calamansi or lemon wedges on the side.
